# Midterms × crypto — single post (with the video)

crypto spent ~$200M on the US midterms and still ended up with a democratic congress

quick context if you don't follow US politics: the midterms are the election halfway through a president's term, when Americans vote for Congress, the people who actually write the laws

crypto went in big this time. the industry has its own super PAC called Fairshake, a political fund that can raise unlimited money to back or attack candidates

together with a few smaller ones, they spent around $200M trying to get crypto-friendly people elected

it mostly didn't work. democrats took both the House and the Senate, and Sherrod Brown, one of crypto's loudest critics, won in Ohio after tens of millions were spent to keep him out

for the industry this means the CLARITY Act, the bill that would finally give crypto clear rules in the US, will probably stall or get rewritten, and Elizabeth Warren is expected to lead the Senate Banking Committee, which decides which financial laws even get a vote

regulation isn't going away, it's just slower and stricter now
