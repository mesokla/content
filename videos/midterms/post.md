# Midterms × crypto — single post (with the video)

crypto went in big on the US midterms this year. the industry has its own super PAC called Fairshake, a political fund that can raise unlimited money to back or attack candidates, and together with a few smaller ones they spent around $200M trying to get crypto-friendly people into Congress

it mostly didn't work. democrats took both the House and the Senate, and Sherrod Brown, one of crypto's loudest critics, won in Ohio even after tens of millions were spent to keep him out

so the CLARITY Act, the bill that was supposed to finally give crypto clear rules in the US, will probably stall, and Elizabeth Warren is expected to run the Senate Banking Committee. the rules are still coming, just slower and a lot stricter than anyone in crypto hoped
